# Recruiting the first ~20 students (DRAFT materials — nothing here has been sent)

Do not contact anyone without deciding who sends it. These are drafts for the founder to edit and send themselves.
**Do not promise real opportunities until real, verified listings are loaded** (see DATA_ENTRY.md). Until then the app only has clearly-labeled fictional samples.

## Before outreach checklist
- [ ] ≥ 30 verified, currently-open DMV listings loaded; samples removed.
- [ ] You tested the full flow on a phone with a fresh email address.
- [ ] Custom SMTP works (a stranger gets the sign-in email within ~1 min, not in spam).
- [ ] Privacy page reviewed; you're comfortable with it being shown to a school.
- [ ] You know what you'll measure (see Analytics): completed profile → saved → opened apply link → self-reported applied, and the "Not a good match?" reasons.

## Email to a school counselor (edit freely)
Subject: Free tool to help [School] students find opportunities they're actually eligible for

Hi [Name],

I'm [name], building OpportunityOS — a free tool for DC/MD/NoVA high-school students. A student enters a short profile (grade, age, ZIP, interests — no street address), and sees internships, scholarships, and programs they qualify for, with the reason for each match and a flag when a requirement needs confirming. Every listing is checked by a person against the organization's official page, with the verification date shown, and students apply on the organization's own site.

I'm looking for ~10–20 students to try it this [month] and tell me what's wrong. Would you be willing to share a short note with students (or let me send you a one-paragraph blurb)? I'm happy to show you the privacy page, answer questions, or walk through it in 10 minutes.

Thanks, [name] · [contact]

## Blurb for students
"Tired of searching 20 websites? OpportunityOS shows internships, scholarships, and programs you actually qualify for — and tells you why. Takes 2 minutes, no password. It's new, so tell me when something's off."

## What to ask testers (5 minutes, in person or by video)
1. "Open it and find something you'd apply to. Talk out loud." (Watch; don't help.)
2. "Was any result wrong or surprising? Why?"  3. "Did you trust the dates? What would make you trust them more?"  4. "Would you come back? What would bring you back?"

## Guardrails
- Students 13+ only; if a school wants a parent notice, provide the privacy page.
- No incentives that depend on applying or reporting "accepted".
- Don't collect feedback by DM that includes personal details; use the app's picklist or a call.
